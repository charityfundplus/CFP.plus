## Scope
This is a **logical research archive inside the sole CFP+ HUB 69**, for evidence-backed research on organizations, technologies and publicly sourced materials associated with the Elon Musk ecosystem. Corporate relationships, legal identities, parentage, source provenance and authority must be individually verified; a common founder/leader is not proof of a legal parent/subsidiary relationship.

## Data direction and isolation
- Approved authoritative CFP+ source → privacy- and license-reviewed outbound immutable snapshot → independently operated research archive.
- Never auto-sync in reverse; never allow deletion, overwrite, corruption or ransomware propagation to archived versions.
- Archive read access must remain possible from verified local snapshots during source outage, subject to independently tested identity, network and key dependencies.
- Preserve canonical IDs, parent relationships and DID as authoritative source metadata, without automatically creating or changing them.
- Derived analysis is a proposal for Human Governance, not an automatic change to authoritative CFP+ records.
