## 2 • Developer allocation rule

1. Sort Tier-1 developers alphabetically A–Z.
2. Direct slots `1–9` are used for the first nine approved developers.
3. Slot `0` is permanently reserved as the **Developer Expansion Link**. It is not assigned to any developer.
4. Developer #10 and later, plus future developers, go under the slot-`0` expansion branch.
5. Tier-2 developers are also listed alphabetically, except an explicit Human Governance placement decision may override ordering without changing the global rule.
6. Do not invent a Canonical ID where allocation has not been approved. Use `Pending Allocation`.

## 7 • CMP work rule

CMP may automatically create inventories, drafts, validation reports and review packages. CMP and participating AI must not:

- self-assign unapproved Canonical IDs;
- reuse an existing ID;
- silently rewrite a locked lineage;
- mark work Executed without output + timestamp + evidence;
- merge, publish or Canonically Lock without Human Governance approval.

## 8 • Required country completion package

A country is ready for review when it has:

- Country identity and Chapter 6 ID;
- alphabetical Developer inventory;
- fixed slot-0 expansion link;
- Tier 1 and Tier 2 placement;
- official source/evidence for each named developer;
- AI inventory under each developer;
- Canonical Link plan;
- conflict/migration log;
- validation result;
- Vietnamese public-layer content.
