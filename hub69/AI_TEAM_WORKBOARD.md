# HUB 69 — AI Team Workboard

**Status:** Active planning board
**Governance:** Human approval required for migration/publish/write operations

## Shared mission

Build and maintain the HUB 69 operating blueprint so AI agents can work in parallel without creating conflicting canonical sources or changing legacy CFP+ assets without authorization.

| AI | Primary lane | Current instruction |
|---|---|---|
| Coordinator | Dispatch / dependencies | Maintain work order and stop gates |
| Content | Content structure | Analyze and prepare; do not migrate |

## Parallel work allowed now

1. Documentation normalization.
2. Cross-reference and link mapping.
3. Evidence collection.
4. Canonical-source candidate analysis.
5. Website build specifications.
6. AI work instructions and status reporting.
7. Repository structure and documentation quality improvements.

## Work requiring explicit Human Governance approval

- Moving or duplicating legacy pages.
- Deleting or renaming legacy content.
- Publishing or unpublishing.
- Selecting a Notion Site Homepage.
- Changing domains.
- Bulk migration.
- Any irreversible or externally visible change.

## Reporting format

Every AI work item should report:

- Objective
- Source/evidence
- Files/pages examined
- Proposed change
- Dependencies/conflicts
- Risk
- Status: `READY`, `BLOCKED`, `REVIEW`, `UNKNOWN`, or `COMPLETE`
- Required Human Decision
