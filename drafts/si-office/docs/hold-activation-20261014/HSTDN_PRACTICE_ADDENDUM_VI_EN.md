## Exercise/source binding • Liên kết bài thực hành và nguồn

Retain real workspace,pair,taskKey,actual sourceRevision and output/checks/feedback/
separate reviewer. A local record key is not a Canonical ID. Hash actual output;
do not fill a missing historical taskKey or re-run an old cohort to hide loss.
Mỗi output cần kỳ vọng/thực tế và thời điểm; genericreflection không thay kết quả.

Use practice-evidence-review.mjs on a copy of an existing export. It validates
mechanical bindings,produces a nonsensitive evidence packet and keeps all
competency states NOT ASSESSED pending official assessor assignment.

## Three concrete SI/AI–HSTDN cases • Ba tình huống

1. Country/Developer missing or conflicting: record the supplied claims and exact
HTTPS primary-source URLs/dates; UNRESOLVED/EVIDENCE_PENDING. Compare country/
developer to the existing Registry inventory. Never infer Parent from name,
numericgap or sourceorder. / Không tự đổiParent khi chưa đủ nguồn.
2. HSTDN legal-parent claim: retain the declarant's authority evidence,legal
entity/jurisdiction,member local keys and proposed relationship separately.
Check member belongs to the authorized office/pair; keep legalRelationship
NOT VERIFIED. A legal-parent-claim is not a Parent allocation.
3. Advanced evidence conflict: retain both artifact versions and hashes tied to
the real taskKey. List missing prerequisites/reviewer authority and source
contradictions. Draft review,source verification and competency are separate.
No payment,real adapter,partnership or source access inferred.

## Worksheet fields • Trường phiếu review

- taskKey/workspace/pair/track/sourceRevision/outputSHA256: actual exported values.
- expected/observed and artifact kind/hash: measured output,not a generic promise.
- claim,value,primarysourceURL,checkedUTC,scope and contradiction.
- declared country/developer/Parent/owner: reference claims; immutable public
ID/Parent/DID/Canonical fields remain untouched.
- sourceVerification/capabilityVerification/legalRelationshipVerification:
unknown or exact scope; no blanket VERIFIED.
- prerequisite artifact references; separate draft reviewer; official assessor
identity/authority/receipt: missing values stay null/PENDING.
- finding→evidence→recommendation→closurecriteria→nextowner(PENDING if unassigned).

The sidecar does not certify arbitrary artifact hash or a claimed assessor.
It detects missing/duplicate/wrong bindings and prepares the packet for the
official reviewer. No old records,audit history,permissions or runtime state
are updated. Three new synthetic-fixture tests are distinct from historical
training/Core/browser/backup tests,which were not rerun.
